# Contact and feedback

Each app MUST link to its internal contact page from the footer, including at
320 and 360 CSS pixels. Opening the page immediately after editing and returning
MUST preserve the latest input. Keep existing published help and support URLs.

Use `apps@haak3.de` for general and privacy enquiries. On the contact page,
render each confirmed profile as an external link with a local decorative icon
and a visible platform name. Keep keyboard focus visible and provide a touch
target of at least 44 × 44 CSS pixels. Use plain links without social embeds.

EduNewsletter has confirmed social profiles. Until its feedback destinations
are assigned, replace the two form placeholders with the app email address.
External profile, form and donation links MUST open in a new tab with
`rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Give the mobile coffee action an accessible name
when its visible label collapses.

The Creator navigation and contact behavior come from SocialMediaCreator and
StoryboardCreator. Feedbackbogen-Generator also uses an internal support page
with a plain donation link. Existing apps can adopt these content and footer
changes within their current framework.
